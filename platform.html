ABYSS — Platform

The Intelligence Layer Between the Ocean and Its Observers.

A unified field intelligence platform connecting autonomous observation, environmental sensing, and adaptive modeling into a living picture of the ocean's state.

What we built

One platform. The entire ocean signal chain.

Abyss is not a data portal. It is an operational intelligence layer — designed to receive, process, and model ocean signals from autonomous field systems, return them as structured field observations, and continuously refine its picture of the ocean as new data arrives.

The platform does not assume the ocean is static between observations. It treats every gap in the record as an inference problem and resolves it using temporal models built on years of longitudinal field data.

How data flows through ABYSS

Every observation enters the platform with a full provenance record — where it came from, when, at what depth, under what conditions, and how confident we are in it.

Model

ML models integrate spatial, temporal, and environmental layers into continuous field representations.

05

Atlas

A continuously updated field intelligence model — accessible to researchers, partners, and field teams.

Core capabilities

Six core capabilities

01
01 · Bathymetry

Multibeam Sonar
Integration

High-resolution swath bathymetry synthesized with overlapping multibeam acoustic coverages to generate real-time 3D spatial seafloor models.

02
02 · Swarm Systems

Autonomous AUV
Coordination

Multi-vehicle autonomous formation maneuvers with dynamic decentralized path recalculation reacting to real-time current velocity and mission gradients.

03
03 · Hydrography

Water Column
Profiling

Continuous vertical characterization of thermocline, halocline, dissolved oxygen, and fluorescence properties sampled synchronously across depth layers.

04
04 · Longitudinal

Temporal Signal
Stack

Time-series telemetry architecture retaining absolute spatio-temporal anchors for raw sensor packets without lossy pre-averaging.

05
05 · Ecological ML

Habitat Change
Detection

Automated anomaly extraction across repeat seasonal transects, detecting micro-shifts in benthic structure, biogenic matter, and acoustic signatures.

06
06 · Interoperability

Open Research
API

Programmatic GraphQL and GeoJSON endpoints allowing research partners to stream continuous observation arrays filtered by geographic bounding box and depth interval.

Deployment model

How a field program works

01

Deploy

Autonomous systems are deployed to the target province with pre-loaded mission parameters and adaptive path planning.

02

Record

Sensors collect continuous, position-indexed signal streams across multiple depths and lateral transects.

03

Model

Data is processed, calibrated, and integrated into the field model — alongside all historical observations from the same region.

04

Deliver

Field teams and partners receive structured results, change summaries, and updated field atlas tiles for their region.
