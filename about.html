ABOUT ABYSS

We Exist to Make the Hidden Ocean Legible.

ABYSS was founded on a simple observation: the ocean generates more signals than we have ever had tools to read.

MISSION

The ocean is the planet's largest unread system. It moves heat, stores carbon, regulates atmosphere, and sustains food webs that underpin nearly all life on earth. Despite this, the deep ocean remains the least mapped, least monitored, and least understood environment on the planet. We are operating from inherited snapshots — isolated surveys, short expeditions, and models built on sparse data — when what the ocean demands is continuous reading.

ABYSS was built to address this gap — not through a better expedition, but through a fundamentally different relationship with time. Longitudinal intelligence means observations that connect across seasons, years, and decades. It means understanding not just what exists at depth, but how it changes, how signals at 2,000 metres relate to surface anomalies weeks later, and how the slow patterns that escape single-mission science can finally be resolved. We build the tools and the infrastructure to make that possible.

Within the scientific community, ABYSS occupies an unusual position: we are neither a pure research institution nor a commercial operator. We exist at the intersection — building field systems rigorous enough to meet scientific standards while deploying them at scales and frequencies that institutional science rarely achieves alone. Our goal is to make that infrastructure available to researchers, regulators, and conservationists who need what the deep ocean is saying, translated into something they can act on.

MILESTONES

Four years in the field.

A record of what we built, where we went, and what we found.

2022
Founded
ABYSS Ocean Intelligence established in San Francisco. Mission: build the first longitudinal field intelligence platform for the deep ocean.
2023
First Deployment
Initial AUV deployment in the Northeast Pacific. 42 days. 180M raw data points. First signal anomalies detected at 2,800m depth.
2024
Deep Ocean Mapping
Full-scale mapping program launches in the Pacific. Eight deployments. First abyssal province mapped at centimeter-scale resolution.
2025
AUV Formation Trials
Three-vehicle coordinated formation operations validated in the Indian Ocean. Foundational for the Autonomous Subsea Survey program.
2026
Signal Intelligence Program
Marine Signal Intelligence program announced. First acoustic anomaly library under construction. Atlantic field operations begin.

OUR VALUES

What we hold ourselves to.

01

Rigor

We build systems designed to be wrong and corrected, not systems designed to appear right. Uncertainty is quantified, not concealed.

02

Context

Every observation is embedded in a field of related conditions. We preserve that field — temperature, pressure, current, time — rather than discard it for a cleaner dataset.

03

Continuity

The ocean does not reset between visits. Neither should the observations. We design for persistence, not expeditions that begin and end with amnesia.
