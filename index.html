intelligence layers

Abyss deploys autonomous systems across unmapped ocean provinces, combining multibeam sonar, optical sensing, and environmental sampling into coherent spatial models of areas no vessel has systematically surveyed.

View Projects

Our subsea intelligence architecture fuses real-time sensor arrays, ROV imagery, and environmental chemical traces into a single spatially indexed field model — built to preserve context instead of isolated observations.

Our Science

Our models connect repeated observations across depth and geography — surfacing weak signals, habitat anomalies, and changing ocean conditions that only become visible when observations accumulate over time.

Full pipeline

01 · Map

Spatial
Observation

ROVs, AUVs, and moored instruments collect position-indexed observations across depth profiles and ocean provinces — building a three-dimensional record of a place over time.

02
02 · Sense

Signal
Acquisition

Physical, chemical, and acoustic signals captured as continuous streams, preserving the temporal context that point samples destroy. Every reading is anchored to when and where it was taken.

03
03 · Model

Intelligence
Integration

Machine learning models fuse spatial, temporal, and environmental layers into coherent field representations of changing ocean systems. Patterns emerge only when observations accumulate.

Field teams, researchers, and partners access a continuously updated picture of the ocean's physical, chemical, and biological state — a living record, not a snapshot.
