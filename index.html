Exploration pipeline

From signal to understanding

The ABYSS pipeline connects the moment of observation to a living picture of the ocean's changing systems.

01 MAP

Spatial Observation

ROVs, AUVs, and moored instruments collect position-indexed observations across depth profiles and ocean provinces.

02 SENSE

Signal Acquisition

Physical, chemical, and acoustic signals captured as continuous streams, preserving temporal context that point samples destroy.

03 MODEL

Intelligence Integration

Machine learning models fuse spatial, temporal, and environmental layers into coherent field representations of changing ocean systems.

04 UNDERSTAND

Living Atlas

Field teams, researchers, and partners access a continuously updated picture of the ocean's physical, chemical, and biological state.

The pipeline is designed around three linked questions: what is changing, where is it changing, and what happens next?
