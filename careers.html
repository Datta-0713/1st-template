ABYSS — Careers

Work at the Edge of the Known Ocean.

ABYSS is a small, rigorous, deeply motivated team. We hire for depth of thinking — not surface credentials.

What it's like to work here

Genuinely hard problems

The ocean does not give up its information easily. We work on problems where the data is sparse, the environment is uncontrolled, and the stakes are real. If you want clean benchmarks, this is not the right place.

Small team, large scope

ABYSS operates across engineering, marine science, machine learning, and field operations. Everyone's work matters. Decisions are made in rooms where the people in those rooms are accountable to the results.

Field-first thinking

We build platforms for people operating in the field — at sea, at depth, in conditions that are not lab conditions. Our standards come from what works in the real environment, not what works in a presentation.

Ocean Data Scientist

Working at ABYSS

Field access

Opportunities to participate in ocean field programs — at sea, at depth, and at the frontier of what is measurable.

Research support

Publication support, conference travel, and a research stipend for continuing education in your field of work.

Small team

No bureaucracy. Your work goes directly into systems that operate in the field. Credit is real and visible.

Competitive package

Salary at market or above, equity participation, comprehensive health coverage, and flexible working arrangements.
