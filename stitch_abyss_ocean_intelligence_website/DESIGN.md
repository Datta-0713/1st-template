## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-precision marine temporal exposomics and ocean intelligence identity. It synthesizes pristine clinical biotechnology with deep oceanographic authority, channeling the editorial rigor, airy composition, and technical credibility of modern frontier biology platforms.

The design movement combines **Clinical Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Glassmorphism**:
- **Pristine & Technical:** Expansive whites, clinical neutral layers, hairline dividers, and surgical data density.
- **Abyssal Depth:** Deep, oceanic navy and teal tones evoking deep-sea zones, light refraction through water, and temporal data mapping.
- **Editorial Typography:** High-contrast scale pairings using ultra-clean grotesk forms with delicate weights for scientific declarations alongside dense, monospaced metadata markers.
- **Fluid Immersion:** Translucent floating navigation surfaces, soft refractions, and gradual ocean-gradient vignettes that blend computational telemetry into high-definition marine media.

## Colors

The color system relies on high-contrast clinical white canvases counterweighted by deep, high-salinity oceanic hues and luminous biophotonic accents.

- **Primary (`#184b66`):** Deep oceanic navy/teal anchoring primary headlines, structural frames, key action states, and high-level telemetry indicators.
- **Secondary (`#0ea5e9`):** Luminous cyan accent representing active sensor telemetry, data highlights, and micro-interactions.
- **Tertiary (`#2c7294`):** Pelagic mid-teal for secondary structural elements, data track lines, and inactive pipeline segments.
- **Neutral (`#64748b`):** Balanced slate gray for technical captions, axis labels, and metadata indicators.
- **Canvas & Surfaces:** Pure clinical white (`#ffffff`) as base background, paired with ultra-light slate tint (`#f8fafc`) for structural cards and deep abyssal darks (`#0c212d` to `#184b66`) for cinematic video masks and dark telemetry sections.
- **Refractive Accents:** Soft light cyan tones (`#e0f2fe` to `#bae6fd`) deployed for badge fills, interactive hover sheens, and pipeline completion states.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes light-weight, scientific editorial display titles with utilitarian sans-serif body text and monospaced technical telemetry.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Headlines):** Used in lightweight (`300`) and regular (`400`) cuts with slight negative letter-spacing for an elegant, clinical-biotech authority that mirrors academic discoveries.
- **Inter (Body):** Selected for high micro-legibility in dense scientific findings, exposomic markers, and multi-paragraph technical research abstracts.
- **JetBrains Mono (Telemetry & Labels):** Applied strictly to exposomic coordinates, biomarker identifiers, depth indicators, temporal timestamps, and step indexes to reinforce computational rigor.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on a 12-column fluid grid system engineered for precision data visualization, clinical workflows, and editorial showcases.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `4rem` lateral canvas margins. Supports asymmetrical split-screen layouts (e.g., 5-column fixed narrative panel alongside a 7-column interactive exposome visualization canvas).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with `1.25rem` gutters and `2.5rem` outer margins. Data tables collapse to scrollable viewports; dual cards transition to single-column blocks.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` lateral safe margins. Navigation shifts to a sticky bottom floating pill or compact glassmorphic header bar.
- **Vertical Spacing Cadence:** High vertical pacing (`space-xl` and multipliers up to `6rem`) between scientific editorial sections creates calm clinical white space, preventing visual fatigue across dense data sets.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through aquatic transparency, ultra-fine boundary borders, and diffused underwater ambient shadows rather than stark elevation planes.

- **Level 1 (Clinical Cards & Panels):** Flat `#f8fafc` surface backed by a hairline border: `1px solid rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.12)`. No shadow, relying on chromatic contrast against pure `#ffffff` canvases.
- **Level 2 (Floating Glass Navigation & Modals):** Translucent backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(180%)`), fill of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)` (or `rgba(12, 33, 45, 0.85)` in dark ocean mode), sealed with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)` and an ambient refraction shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(24, 75, 102, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Hovering Action States & Flyouts):** Soft directional lift with abyssal cyan diffusion: `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.15)`.
- **Media Seamless Borders:** Boundary containers over ocean video or temporal cartography use vertical CSS mask gradients (`linear-gradient(to bottom, rgba(0,0,0,1) 80%, rgba(0,0,0,0) 100%)`) or multi-stop cyan-to-transparent edge vignettes that dissolve scientific media directly into off-white card backgrounds.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a balanced geometry (`roundedness: 2`) that pairs soft laboratory precision with full pill radii for temporal tags and interactive triggers.

- **Standard Surfaces & Cards:** Standardized at `0.5rem` (`rounded-md`) up to `1rem` (`rounded-lg`) for large research modules, avoiding overly playful bubbly curves while eliminating harsh industrial corners.
- **Pills & Badges:** Fixed at `9999px` (`rounded-full`) for all metadata badges, status chips, and primary action buttons.
- **Interactive Triggers:** Circular enclosures (`50%` radius) reserved for accordion toggle icons, pagination, and circular directional arrow buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#184b66` pill button with white text (`Inter`, 14px, medium). Includes a trailing circular glyph housing a forward-angled arrow (`18px` white circle with `#184b66` icon). On hover, background shifts to `#1e5977` and the circular arrow rotates 45 degrees smoothly.
- **Secondary Glass Action:** Translucent white pill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`), `1px solid rgba(24, 75, 102, 0.2)`, slate-cyan text `#184b66`. Hover trigger deepens border opacity to `0.5` with subtle cyan glow.

### Badges & Research Chips
- **Clinical Biomarker Pill:** Micro-height (`24px`), `rounded-full`, soft cyan background (`rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.08)`), crisp border (`1px solid rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.25)`), typography set in uppercase `JetBrains Mono` (`10px`, weight 500, `#0284c7`).
- **Telemetry Status Chip:** Includes a leading `6px` biophotonic pulsing indicator dot (green for stable data streams, cyan for active analysis, amber for calibration).

### Floating Header & Dropdown Navigation
- **Floating Bar:** Island structure anchored `1.5rem` below the top viewport, centered, max-width `1120px`, height `64px`, `rounded-full`. Translucent surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`) with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and hairline border (`rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.15)`).
- **Glass Dropdown Panel:** Detached glass card (`rounded-2xl`, `p-6`) positioned below nav items with subtle spring entrance animation, displaying categorized exposome matrices and temporal deep-dive links with metadata sublabels.

### Interactive Accordions
- **Research Disclosure Tile:** Card-like horizontal rows partitioned by hairline borders (`rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.12)`). Clean Plus Jakarta Sans heading with a trailing circular toggle button.
- **Expand/Collapse Trigger:** `32px` circular neutral button with an inset `+` line icon that morphs via CSS rotation (`transform: rotate(45deg)`) to an `×` when expanded, triggering smooth grid-template-rows expansion of content.

### Clinical Pipeline Progress Indicator
- **Multi-Stage Telemetry Track:** Horizontal sequence connected by a hairline progress bar (`2px`).
- **Pipeline Nodes:** Circular step markers (`28px`). Completed steps feature a solid `#184b66` or `#0ea5e9` fill with a white micro checkmark icon. Active stages show a dual concentric ring with a pulsing core. Upcoming stages feature a hollow border (`rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.3)`) and monospaced step digit. Stage titles sit beneath each node in `12px` font with monospaced temporal readouts (e.g., `T-00:48H`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Scientific Field Inputs:** Clean `44px` height, `rounded-lg`, background `#f8fafc`, border `1px solid rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.2)`. On focus, border sharpens to `#0ea5e9` with a subtle box-shadow ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Custom `18px` controls styled with `#184b66` fill upon selection, featuring precise 1.5px checkmark strokes and zero ambient blur for maximum clinical clarity.

### Cards & Media Blends
- **Exposome Data Cards:** Elevated white cards with `#f8fafc` interior data wells. Hairline borders and optional gradient top-bars (`2px` height gradient from `#0ea5e9` to `#184b66`).
- **Ocean Media Vignette Containers:** Structural containers holding marine loop video or bathymetric heatmaps utilize radial fade masks (`radial-gradient(ellipse at center, black 60%, transparent 100%)`) allowing temporal visualizations to float organically within the clinical white canvas.
