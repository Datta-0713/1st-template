Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

ABYSS Ocean Intelligence ("ABYSS", "we", "us", or "our") collects information you provide directly to us when you use our website, request access to our platform, or contact us. This includes contact information such as your name, email address, institution, and the content of any messages you send.

We also collect certain information automatically when you visit our site, including your IP address, browser type, pages viewed, and the referring site. This information is collected through standard server logs and does not identify you personally.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to respond to inquiries and requests, provide access to our platform for authorized research partners, communicate updates relevant to our field programs, and improve our website and platform experience.

We do not use your information to serve third-party advertising, and we do not sell or rent your information to any third party.

3. Information Sharing

We share your information only with service providers who assist us in operating our website and conducting our business — including hosting providers and email service platforms. These providers are contractually required to use your information only to provide services to us and may not use it for their own purposes.

We may also disclose information when required by law or legal process, or to protect the rights and safety of ABYSS and others.

4. Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies to improve your browsing experience and understand how visitors use our site. These include essential cookies required for site operation and analytics cookies that help us understand usage patterns. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features of the site may not function properly as a result.

We do not use third-party advertising cookies or social media tracking pixels.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, comply with legal obligations, and resolve any disputes. Contact information submitted through our website is typically retained for up to three years unless you request deletion earlier.

Scientific and field data collected through the ABYSS platform is retained indefinitely as part of our longitudinal ocean intelligence record, in accordance with our data stewardship commitments to partner institutions.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information held by ABYSS. You may also request that we restrict processing of your information or object to its use in certain circumstances. To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@abyss.ocean.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

7. Security

We implement technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this privacy policy or our data practices, please contact us at privacy@abyss.ocean or write to ABYSS Ocean Intelligence, 415 Mission Street, Suite 1200, San Francisco, CA 94105.
