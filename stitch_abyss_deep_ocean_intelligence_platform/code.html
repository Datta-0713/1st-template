Mapping ocean physiology over time

The ABYSS platform maps cyclical growth rings in abyssal biostructures and benthic mineral horizons to reveal thousands of chemical features across multi-year intervals. A single temporal acoustic assay yields as much longitudinal data as hundreds of deep-sea sampling expeditions, illuminating how ocean biosystems evolve over millennia.

Proprietary autonomous sensorics

Leveraging pressure-resilient micro-robotics and high-frequency subsea spectrometry, our autonomous probes isolate complex pelagic signatures in real time. We process bathymetric readouts through our onboard neural compute engine, isolating molecular markers of deep-trench hydrothermal activity, biogenic carbon sinks, and unseen geothermal flows.

Featured projects

ABYSS has developed the world’s first autonomous deep-trench acoustic sensor array — BathySense-1. Capable of continuous sub-abyssal deployment at depths beyond 9,000 meters, our sensors capture real-time geodynamic and biochemical equilibrium markers.
